# Tintako Launcher Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026. Applies to Tintako Launcher 1.0-beta5 and later (package `com.tintako.launcher`).

## The short version
Tintako Launcher works entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics, and it never sends anything anywhere.

## Who is responsible
Tintako, Croatia. Contact: app@tintako.com.

## What the app stores, and where
Only on your device, in the app's private storage: your settings, the order of your apps, which apps you hid or locked, and the wallpaper. If you choose a folder of pictures, the launcher only reads it; the picture you pick is kept as a converted grey copy, and the folder itself is never changed. Nothing is copied to backups or moved to a new device by the app. Uninstalling the app removes it all.

## Permissions, and what each is for
- **Notification access**, only if you allow it: the launcher counts notifications per app to show badges on icons. It uses only the app name and the count; it never reads or stores the title or text of a notification. When you choose Clear notifications in an icon's menu, it removes that app's notifications, as swiping them away would.
- **Read weather data** (`com.mudita.weather.PERMISSION_READ_WEATHER_DATA`): only if you turn weather on, the launcher reads the current weather that the phone's Weather app offers to other apps. The launcher itself fetches nothing.
- **Wallpaper folder**, only if you choose one in the phone's own picker: read access to that one folder, until you remove it in Settings.
- **Biometric** (`USE_BIOMETRIC`): the phone's own PIN or fingerprint check, only for parental control. The launcher never sees your PIN or fingerprint. An own PIN for parental control is kept on this phone only, as a salted hash, never as the number.
- The Android system adds one internal permission (`DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`) that only protects the app's own internal messages.

## What leaves, and only when you ask
Nothing. The Tip jar page shows a QR code and the address `ko-fi.com/tintako`; the app itself never opens it.

## Who else receives anything
No one. The app connects to no servers.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)
The app processes no personal data on Tintako's behalf. What it keeps stays on your device under your control.

## Your rights
Because Tintako receives no data from the app, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete everything by uninstalling the app. You can complain to your national data protection authority (in Croatia, AZOP).

## Children
Tintako Launcher is suitable for everyone. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is published here and in the app (Settings → Privacy), with a new date.
